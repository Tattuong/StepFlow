## Title
StepFlow

## Short description
Branching checklists that pick the next step for you.

## Full description
StepFlow walks you through a job one step at a time. Unlike a normal checklist, the answer you pick decides what comes next — a room check before checkout, an equipment handover, or packing a shipment.

Create steps with Yes/No or several answers. Important steps ask you to confirm before you continue. Pause a session and pick it up later. When you finish, export a summary of the steps you completed and the issues you found.

Three playbooks are included. Add your own with a form. Rules run on this phone. No account.

Looks, keep-screen-on, tap sound, and StepFlow Pro (more than 8 workflows you create) unlock with coins from the daily reward or 10 coin packs. Remove ads and StepFlow Pro are one-time unlocks.
