Privacy Policy – StepFlow

Last updated: October 2026

StepFlow is a private, on-device branching checklist. Your playbooks, sessions, coins, and shop unlocks stay on this phone. We do not create an account or upload workflow text.

Data stored on device

  • Playbooks you create, session logs, and recorded issues
  • Coins, owned shop items, and the active look
  • Purchase tokens needed to restore Remove ads and StepFlow Pro

Third-party services

  • Google Play Billing – coin packs, Remove ads, and StepFlow Pro (handled by Google)
  • Remote config – GET https://api2.blwsmartware.net/N297.json to read the disable field (no personal data is sent)

We do not sell personal data. Contact the developer through the Play Store listing.
